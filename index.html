Frequently Asked Questions

What is Algo Trading or Algorithmic Trading?
Algo trading, or algorithmic trading, is a form of systematic trading where you create pre-defined rules for entries, exits, and risk management and code them so a computer can execute trades automatically on your behalf. It removes emotions from trading and ensures speed, consistency, and discipline in execution. It typically follows a 5-step framework: Ideation, Backtesting, Optimization, Forward Testing, and Automation.
How does Algo Trading work?
Algo trading works through automated engines that receive and process live market data in real time. These systems use predefined rules, mathematical models, and technical indicators to identify trading opportunities. Once a specific condition — such as a price level, trend pattern, or volatility threshold — is met, the algorithm automatically generates and executes trade orders without manual intervention.
Is Algo Trading Profitable?
Yes, algo trading can be profitable, but consistent profitability depends on factors such as your strategy's logic, risk management, market conditions, and disciplined execution. A systematic, process-first approach — backtesting, optimizing, and automating strategies with data-driven precision — helps remove emotional decision-making and improves consistency over time.
Is Algo Trading legal in India?
Yes, algo trading is completely legal in India, as long as it follows the regulations set by SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India). Retail traders can use approved APIs provided by their brokers to automate trades on authorized platforms within SEBI's guidelines. Fully automated non-intervention systems require exchange approval, while semi-automated or user-authorized executions are fully compliant.
